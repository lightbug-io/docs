# Chasm Share

The Chasm Share application is a web-based tool that allows users to view public shared messaging from Lightbug devices.

<DeviceCompatibilityV3 fullWidth/>

Upon access, you'll be presented with a form to enter a device ID and share code, which are generated when [creating a share](./creating-a-share).

![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/03/chrome_ZVCEQhVHsC.png)

If you are accessing a share via a valid direct URL, you will end up on the share view page, which displays the shared data.

![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/03/chrome_j7qoWf3Ehb.png)

For a share with a future end time, its snapshot becomes available only after that time. Opening the link early shows a countdown; when it ends, the viewer retries preparation. The share is a snapshot, not a live stream.

![Share viewer showing a countdown until a future-ended snapshot becomes available](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/share-future/ccd25577-f46c-48da-9a8f-e7c593420bd5.png)
