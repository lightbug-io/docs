# Powered Vehicle RTK (VTRTK)

A powered RTK tracker for vehicles, with advanced GNSS and RTK capabilities.

Currently available for demo, with the form factor of a [VT3](/devices/vehicle/) device and high-accuracy RTK positioning. It is a separate hardware model from the [RH2 handheld](/devices/rtk/handheld/); do not assume identical LEDs, wireless features or application support.

## LED indicators

VTRTK uses four indicators for the standard battery and ignition/charging display, not RH2’s RGB status colours. See the [VTRTK LED guide](/guides/rtk-leds#vehicle-rtk-vtrtk) for the matching patterns and revision caveats.
