# Why are there two location points?

**`Target Audience:`** First time users  
**`Difficulty:`** Easy  
**`Prerequisites:`** A Lightbug account and access to the mobile app  

## Guide

There will be two [location points](../terminology/points.md) if there has not been an accurate `location update` in more than `two days`. The most recent location is *not* always the most accurate. This accuracy depends on the last type of location update your device has emitted. [GPS](../terminology/positioning/gnss.md) updates are more accurate than [WIFI](../terminology/positioning/wifi.md) or [Bluetooth](../devices/capabilities/ble-scanning.md) updates.

::: tabs
== Desktop
#### Dashboard  
Each device shows `two dots`, and in this case, the `oldest` is shown here in `green` with the `most recent approximate` one in `grey`. The `grey dot` will automatically display `basic information` about the location:  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/two-location-points/Desktop-1-91c7f01c-3a4b-4672-b1ed-f4115d19592c.png)  
#### More Information  
`Clicking` on the `grey dot` or pressing "SHOW" displays a `yellow question mark` icon. Hovering the mouse over this icon will give you more information on the accuracy of this approximation:  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/two-location-points/Desktop-2-0fc25614-89aa-4509-afad-08b07867d3f2.png)  
Clicking on the green (older) dot brings up some new icons:  
- The `red cross` closes the other icons  
- The `cog icon` displays the `current device settings`  
- Hovering the `clock icon` notifies you that the location may not be accurate and a more recent one is available  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/two-location-points/Desktop-4-41d47953-7625-4c78-9d3c-b617d27ee53f.png)  
`Clicking` on the `grey dot` will make it disappear until "SHOW" is pressed again:  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/two-location-points/Desktop-3-96dce91d-fc37-46cc-89ae-8a4451f40dba.png)  
`Clicking` on the `show button` will `bring the grey approximate location back` and will pan it to the centre of the screen, displaying the `date and time` of the location point:  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/two-location-points/Desktop-1-91c7f01c-3a4b-4672-b1ed-f4115d19592c.png)
== Mobile  
#### Dashboard
Each device shows `two dots`, the `most recent` is shown here in `orange`, and the `older` one in `grey`:  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/10/docs-guides-fresh-grab/2043268582248-XMGpwiriw5_B0jPA3F4aUMCIRLtB-vL0eQ.png) 
#### Basic Information  
`Tapping and holding` on the `orange dot` will display `basic information` about the location:  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/10/docs-guides-fresh-grab/2043268582462-azCE3ud-C3MdKNLFd1F10xdpvnvzku7kJg.png) 
#### More Information  
`Tapping` on the `orange dot` displays several icons:  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/10/docs-guides-fresh-grab/2043268582541-jG-Mhf5f3_syZGUxZZ42wBRwBeqOR2wO5A.png) 
You can tap on each icon to display `more in-depth information` about the location point:  
- The `blue icon` displays information about the `Bluetooth beacon`  
- The `yellow hourglass icon` displays the `age of the location point`  
- The `yellow question mark icon` displays information about the `accuracy` of the location  
- The `cog icon` displays the `current device settings`  
- The `red cross icon` closes the other icons  
---
`Tapping` on the `grey dot` will pan the `older location` to the centre of the screen and display the `location information` at the `bottom of the screen` in black:  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/10/docs-guides-fresh-grab/2043265375984-YQ7I5NVTBatZnY67KEkDeABQPjRoa9XA-w.png) 
`Tapping` on the `show button` will `turn the location point red` and will pan it to the centre of the screen, displaying the `Confidence, tolerance, date and time` of the location point:  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/10/docs-guides-fresh-grab/2043265375975-ZxdeJdnOcqxzvEeZtmv7gmO9-DJZIV1uog.png)
