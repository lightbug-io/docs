# How do I pair the tracker and my phone?

- **Target Audience**: First time users
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Prerequisites**: A Lightbug account and access to the mobile app or web portal

## Guide  

Pairing your Lightbug tracker to your smartphone allows for features such as `Bluetooth safe zone` and `ranging functionality`.  
Find out how to pair below:  

1. Open the `LightBug App` on your smartphone and press the `drop down arrow`
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043239747024/original/WSGKePEK1BvfJobgbZoL8xmonUltHFS_Xg.png){width=250}  

2. Select the device you would like to pair
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043239748333/original/EBq3_YLRFHN_oFMFbd1qh7RQ_KITmF1Dng.png){width=250}  

3. Press `'scan'`
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043239750665/original/fvWQjNigBIHcfBUXEQQkJZKzyIPHGnEmoA.png){width=250}  

4. A popup page will appear. Make a note of the `passkey` and press `'Pair'`  
When prompted enter the `passkey` and wait for the device to pair, this can take up to `30 seconds`
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043239752012/original/OyvOKuzrfXNbtNCfUlo7GcCtbHva1yN04g.png){width=250}  

**`Your device is now paired!`**  

If pairing fails, follow the additional steps below to manually pair the devices from your smartphone's Bluetooth settings:  

5. Make a note of the passkey and then go to your smartphone's `Bluetooth settings` and `select your device`  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043239767488/original/Y4OSeqbNnoekYkd59-QV_yIjVAevghJKhw.png){width=250} 

**Hint:** The device name will start with 'LB' 

6. On the pop up page, enter the noted `passkey` and press `'pair'`  
The tracker should now show as `connected` on the Bluetooth settings page
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043239759717/original/f-1oD5fXZXsz17ZYpqeoP1LjWzGHBLs0VQ.png){width=250}  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043239770652/original/o4M2MOTslBiTiReGblNmDPcq1uWNFmMpGQ.png){width=250}  

7. `Force close` the LightBug App and `restart`. The tracker will now show as `connected` on the App
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043239774737/original/cGZri3enO8dDsoyj8iVM0rVrj2k95lTcuA.png){width=250}  

Your Lightbug tracker is now paired with your smartphone.
If you're have any difficulty pairing, please [let us know!](https://lightbug.io/contact/)
