# How to Create a Home Wi-Fi Safe-Zone

::: info
**You will need to be on the Unlimited Data Plan (as this allows for motion detection) to use this feature.**
:::  

**`Target Audience:`** First time users  
**`Difficulty:`** Easy  
**`Prerequisites:`** A Lightbug account and access to the mobile app or web portal  

## Guide  
The benefit of setting up a `home Wi-fi safe-zone` over a `GPS safe-zone` is that the device will check approx. `every 5 minutes` whether the device is in range of the `Wi-Fi`. If the device `is not`, you will be sent a `notification`. Whereas, if the device leaves the `GPS safe-zone`, the notification will be sent at the `next location update`. 
>> 
To set up your `home Wi-Fi safe-zone`, follow the steps below:  

::: tabs
== Desktop
#### Step 1  
Select `'Settings'` in the menu on the top right of the screen  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/wifi-safe-zone-desktop/Wifi-Safe-Zone-Desktop-1-902fa5ad-caf1-4949-a2fe-3758bca01ac9.png) 
#### Step 2  
Locate the `Safe-zone Setup` box
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/wifi-safe-zone-desktop/Wifi-Safe-Zone-Desktop-2-47be2e2f-203b-4606-a6ec-5a3784d16163.png)  
#### Step 3  
Turn on the `'Ignore motion when near home Wi-Fi'` setting  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/wifi-safe-zone-desktop/Wifi-Safe-Zone-Desktop-3-655994fc-8411-4ef7-b660-77c39e25d516.png) 
#### Step 4  
Enter the name of the Wi-Fi network  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/wifi-safe-zone-desktop/Wifi-Safe-Zone-Desktop-4-725a1e5b-6146-4424-878a-4c8aaecc0072.png) 
#### Step 5  
Select `'Save’` 
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/wifi-safe-zone-desktop/Wifi-Safe-Zone-Desktop-5-e1505755-3427-4348-938f-b1ed43c809f4.png)
== Mobile
#### Step 1  
Open the Menu and select `'Settings'`  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/wifi-safe-zone-app/Wifi-Safe-Zone-App-1-841b5f0d-fdda-432a-882a-42a753b3009c.jpg) 
#### Step 2  
Scroll to the bottom of the `'Settings'` page until you find the `Safe-zone Setup` field  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/wifi-safe-zone-app/Wifi-Safe-Zone-App-2-2b27d049-a622-4f13-885e-81d8b377be31.jpg) 
#### Step 3  
Turn on the `'Ignore motion when near home Wi-Fi'` setting  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/wifi-safe-zone-app/Wifi-Safe-Zone-App-3-b7018ed5-835e-4885-8fbc-d15579917e19.jpg)
#### Step 4  
Enter the name of the Wi-Fi network  
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/wifi-safe-zone-app/Wifi-Safe-Zone-App-4-d4d69dc4-9a04-42f2-b804-078531f400f0.jpg)
#### Step 5  
Select `'Save’` 
![](https://upload.r2.lb.chasm.cloud/2026/11/wifi-safe-zone-app/Wifi-Safe-Zone-App-5-3059a403-4b42-486c-9556-f91fe3144421.jpg)
:::

Your `home Wi-Fi safe-zone` has now been set up.  

::: info
**To apply the new settings immediately, press the button on your tracker. If you don't have your tracker to hand, the tracker will update its settings the next time it is scheduled to send a location update.**
:::
