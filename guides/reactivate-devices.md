# How do I reactivate my Lightbug devices
- **Target Audience**: First time users
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Prerequisites**: A Lightbug account and access to the mobile app or web portal

## Guide

If you have deactivated your device (cancelled your subscription), you can reactivate it at any time from the Lightbug App, just follow the simple steps below:  
1. Open the Lightbug App or go to the Web Portal (https://lightbug.cloud/)  
2. Go to 'Settings', then to the 'Data Plan' page  
3. In the 'Devices' section, find the tracker you would like to reactivate, and click 'activate'  
4. The pop up box will show you a selection of billing plans that you can pick from. Select a plan for the device.  

For an overview of the available plan tiers, see [Plans](/terminology/plans).

(https://account.lightbug.cloud/)
Your device is now reactivated and ready to use!  
If you want to update the settings, we recommend referring to our User Manual 
(https://lightbug.io/user_manual.pdf).

Still need assistance, please reach out to us (https://lightbug.io/contact/).
