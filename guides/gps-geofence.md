# How to create a GPS Geofence (Safe-Zone)  

- **Target Audience**: First time users  
- **Difficulty**: Easy  
- **Prerequisites**: A Lightbug account and access to the mobile app or web portal  

## Guide  

1. Open the `Lightbug App` or go to the [Web Portal](https://lightbug.cloud/)
2. Go to `'Account'`, then to the `'Geofences'` page
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204216556/original/2dC3IUjgO8jYbGuLnfGHTzPhHXy-X6no9w.png){width=250}  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204216869/original/AAImc6eEFiQMIlfdICMkVTiRsrEeMHtpaA.png){width=250}  
3. Click on the `'plus'` icon
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204218783/original/2Lbao4mKDnl9awiT4Dy2aDHkuNAVCEfw6Q.png){width=250}  
4. `Configuration` section
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204221908/original/AvCRigshcKZiPiY3KfvkUpfzWl13VzgUsw.png){width=250}  

**`Name:`** Here you should choose the most `appropriate name` for your geofence, this name will be displayed on `every notification` you get for this alert.  
>>
**`Alert:`** Choose to be notified/alerted when the device:  
* Is `outside` the geofence  
* Is `inside` the the geofence  
* Crosses the `boundary` of the geofence  
>>
**`Additional Conditions:`** You can add `conditions` where an alert will only be triggered if these conditions are met. These conditions include `speed` (for select tracker models) and `time`.  
>>
**`Notification Delivery:`** Choose how you like to be `notified` of the alert. This can be updated on the `Settings` tab.  
>>
5. Devices section  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204229303/original/MIIGqdpF8FM8m_iwAI2LnlOerSxfEL5C-A.png){width=250}  
Here you can enable the alert for all devices on your account. Alternatively, you can enable the alert for `specific trackers` only by selecting the required tracker. This tracker will be added to the selection.  

6. Boundary section  
![Image Missing]()  
Here is where you `create` the geofence, just follow the simple steps below:  
    * `Locate the area` you would like the geofence to be. You can use the `search bar` to help you with this ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204237872/original/4rnh86EToLN2Nx3kg-XgsWe05oU-J3Lcxg.png){width=250}  
    * Choose the `shape` of geofence ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204237873/original/y-RaQBtDD9kEj8msDTrwnaLFZi7ydbasPA.png){width=250}  
    * `Tap the Map` to add points and create your geofence. The `safe zone` can be a standard or `custom irregular` shape by joining up the points ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204233961/original/7LfpUQiSxCefEVTgSjZTpNiV5c35IOH3Zg.png){width=250}  
    ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204234284/original/AJ5A1QAgKQjP4xdUHbzJ1whgF41GvzlsLg.png){width=250}  
    ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204234905/original/9wJd6glClGimedOiOASTxdIcef1vMrgzlQ.png){width=250}
    * Click the first point in the geofence to confirm the shape. The geofence will turn blue to confirm that it is now saved
    ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/2043204239767/original/4aDRnK_hQvCiDXXQJ8NIB_OqB5gxU33H_g.png){width=250}  

The safe zone has now been set up. There are a few things to note:  
* When setting up this type of `safe-zone`, it is advisable to make it at least `50m by 50m` to account for `GPS inaccuracies`  
* When the device `leaves` the `geofence` the `notification` will be sent at the `next location update`  
* To `apply the new settings immediately`, press the `button` on your tracker. If you don't have your tracker to hand, the tracker will update its settings the `next time` it is scheduled to send a `location update`.

If you have any additional questions about setting up safe-zones, don't be afraid to [reach out to us!](https://thelightbug.com/support.html)
